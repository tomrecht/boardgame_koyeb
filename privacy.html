Privacy Policy

Quahuru · last updated 27 August 2026

Quahuru collects no personal data, has no accounts, shows no advertising, and sends nothing about you anywhere. The game runs entirely on your device.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no profile, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. No information about you or your play is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

The computer opponent runs on your device

Quahuru's opponent is a neural network that is downloaded once with the game and then runs locally, on your device. Your moves are never sent to a server to be answered. After the first load the game plays fully offline.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your preferences so it can remember them next time. These are held in your browser's or the app's own local storage, on your device only, and are never transmitted:

  • your chosen theme, difficulty and rule options
  • display settings such as fullscreen
  • a cached copy of the game's own files, so it loads and plays offline

Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, removes all of this permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children

Quahuru is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The game bundles two open-source libraries — Phaser and ONNX Runtime Web — which run locally as part of the app. Neither contacts any server. No other third-party code, service or SDK is included.
